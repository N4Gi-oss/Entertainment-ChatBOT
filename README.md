#WE Entertainment Services Chatbot 🟣

A Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) customer support chatbot for Telecom Egypt (WE), built on the Entertainment services category (WATCH IT, Call Tone, WE Sports, Play & Win).

The bot scrapes real service pages from te.eg, builds a searchable knowledge base, and answers customer questions in Arabic or English — grounded only in the scraped content, with no hallucinated prices or codes.
